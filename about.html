About Golden Scepter

A Lagos school built on one idea: every child carries a gift, and our job is to help them rule their future well.

Our story

Golden Scepter began in 2008 with 46 pupils and three classrooms in Ikeja. Parents asked us to grow with their children, so we added a Primary school in 2011 and our College in 2016. Today we are home to more than 1,200 learners, all taught by teachers who love their work.

2008. Nursery school opens with 46 pupils.

2011. Primary school launches.

2016. College opens with JSS 1 to SS 3.

2020. Computer lab and online learning platform introduced.

2024. New science block and sports field completed.

Our mission

To give every child a sound education, strong character and the confidence to serve their community and the world.


Our vision

To be Lagos's most trusted school for academic excellence and moral leadership.
