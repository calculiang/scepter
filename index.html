Three schools, one family

Your child can stay with us from age 2 to university entry, in one safe and familiar community.

🧸

Nursery School

Ages 2 to 5. Play-based learning, phonics, early numeracy and creative arts in a warm, secure setting.

📚

Primary School

Primary 1 to 6. A blend of the Nigerian and Cambridge-informed curriculum, with strong reading, maths and ICT.

🎓

College

JSS 1 to SS 3. Science, Arts and Commercial tracks with WAEC, NECO and JAMB preparation.

Why parents choose us

Caring, qualified teachers

Small classes of 25 or fewer, with a teacher and an assistant in every Nursery class.

Safe campus

Gated, CCTV-monitored grounds, a school clinic with a resident nurse and secure school buses.

Modern facilities

Science laboratories, a computer lab, a library, a sports field and a creative arts studio.

Character and faith

Daily assembly, moral instruction and mentoring that build honesty, respect and service.
