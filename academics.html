Academics and admissions

A clear path from age 2 to SS 3, with the right support at every stage.

Nursery School (ages 2 to 5)

Our Nursery blends play with structure. Children build language, number sense, motor skills and social confidence through songs, stories, sensory play and outdoor time.

  • Classes: Playgroup, Pre-Nursery, Nursery 1 and 2
  • Phonics, early numeracy, Bible and Quran stories
  • Art, music, rhymes and free play
  • Daily written feedback to parents

Primary School (Primary 1 to 6)

We follow the Nigerian curriculum enriched with Cambridge-informed methods, so pupils read fluently, think clearly and are ready for Common Entrance and College.

  • English, Mathematics, Basic Science, Social Studies
  • Verbal and Quantitative Reasoning, Computer Studies
  • French, Yoruba, Civic Education, Creative Arts
  • Weekly reading club and termly project fairs

College (JSS 1 to SS 3)

Students sit BECE in JSS 3, then choose Science, Arts or Commercial in SS 1. Teachers run extra lessons and mock exams to prepare for WAEC, NECO and JAMB.

  • Core: English, Mathematics, Civic Education, ICT
  • Sciences: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Further Maths
  • Arts and Commercial: Literature, Government, Economics, Accounting
  • Career guidance and university placement support

How to apply

  • Fill in the enquiry form on our Contact page, or visit the school.
  • Collect and return the admission form with a birth certificate and two passport photographs.
  • Your child sits a short assessment (a friendly interview for Nursery).
  • Receive your admission letter, pay fees and complete registration.

Fees and sessions

Fees differ by class and are paid termly. The school year has three terms (September, January and April). Siblings receive a discount. Contact the bursar for the current fee schedule.
